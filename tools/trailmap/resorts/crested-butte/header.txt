// Two panels: the map, and its Teocalli Bowls inset (the High Lift's bowls,

// Crystal and Middle Championship (black, on the Crystal and Championship
// lines). Not on the map: Breakfast Trail, Fallaway, Middle International,
// Short Cut, Upper Roller Coaster, and the three conveyors' beginner areas.
// The main map's lines are its own outlines, named by the name printed in a
// gap of each or by its symbol, or settled on crops (decisions.py). The
// Extremes' open faces, bowls, chutes and glades print no line: markers at
// their names, and the inset's runs at their numbers. Area elevations: the
